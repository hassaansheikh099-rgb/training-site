## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a bold, futuristic, and high-impact identity for Pakistan's premier vocational and advanced tech education institute. Drawing from the dynamic momentum of the crimson isometric arrow mark, the visual language balances disciplined tech minimalism with high-energy cyber-physical accents. 

### Target Audience & Emotional Intent
- **Audience:** Aspiring software engineers, AI specialists, creative technologists, corporate upskillers, and institutional partners seeking career transformation.
- **Emotional Response:** Confidence, cutting-edge technical authority, urgency, high momentum, and prestige. It replaces standard low-contrast educational templates with a mission-control, high-performance ecosystem.

### Design Movement & Aesthetic Archetype
- **Dark-Mode High-Contrast Minimal Glassmorphism:** Deep space zinc and slate substrates paired with optical laser red/crimson accents, subtle neon diffusion, polished frosted acrylics, precision borders, and razor-sharp typographic structures.
- **Visual Rhythm:** Crisp grid boundaries, data-dense stat counters, luminous badge indicators ("LIVE", "AI-Integrated"), and tactile micro-surfaces that elevate trust credentials and job placement outcomes.

## Colors

The color palette centers around a nocturnal deep slate/zinc environment offset by radiant crimson and neon rose accents drawn directly from the 3D brand arrow emblem.

### Color Hierarchy & Roles
- **Primary (`#E11D48` / `crimson-glow`):** Applied to primary actions, interactive hovers, metric counter highlights, energetic focus states, and illuminated indicator badges.
- **Secondary (`#94A3B8`):** Crisp cool metallic slate used for supporting typography, structural delimiters, subheaders, and secondary metadata.
- **Tertiary (`#0284C7`):** Electric tech cyan reserved for specialized computational/AI badges, data visualization accents, and secondary platform cues.
- **Neutral (`#090D16` / `surface-canvas`):** Ultra-deep, blue-tinted void black that gives content high optical clarity and isolates neon red radiance without eye strain.

### Functional States & Lighting Rules
- Dark-mode is the native, canonical state across all surfaces.
- Never use pure desaturated `#000000` for cards; rely on `surface-card` (`#0D1322`) to preserve depth layering.
- Live badges and placement counters utilize soft outward red diffusion (`box-shadow: 0 0 20px rgba(225, 29, 72, 0.35)`).

## Typography

Typography establishes an assertive, modern tech voice. The system pairs **Plus Jakarta Sans** for headlines and running copy with **Geist** for precise computational micro-labels, metrics, badges, and interface data tags.

### Hierarchy & Editorial Rules
- **Display & Section Titles:** Emphasize bold weights with tight negative letter tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) to deliver modern industrial impact. Key nouns and tech accelerators (e.g., "AI", "Bootcamp", "Career") may carry linear gradient fills shifting from `#FFFFFF` down to `#E11D48`.
- **Labels & System Chips:** Powered by `Geist` with uppercase or semi-expanded letter spacing for technical clarity in status indicators (`LIVE`, `CAMPUS OPEN`, `85% PLACEMENT`).
- **Readability:** Body text defaults to slate-tinted white (`#F8FAFC` on body-lg and `#94A3B8` on body-sm) to guarantee WCAG AAA contrast ratios on deep zinc backdrops.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an adaptive 12-column fluid grid system engineered for dense technical curricula, multi-campus listings, and statistical conversion triggers.

### Grid & Canvas Structure
- **Desktop (>= 1280px):** 12 columns with a max content width of `1280px`, `1.5rem` (`24px`) gutters, and `2rem` (`32px`) external canvas margins.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8 columns, `1.25rem` (`20px`) gutters, and `1.5rem` (`24px`) margins. Cards adapt from 3 columns down to 2.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `1rem` (`16px`) gutters, and `1.25rem` (`20px`) outer margin with edge-clamped horizontal scroll ribbons for partner credentials and certification badges.

### Spacing Density Principles
- **Macro Spacing:** Use `space-2xl` (`64px`) between distinct content sections (e.g., between Hero, Bootcamp Tracks, and Placement Stats).
- **Component Interior Spacing:** Cards maintain strict internal padding of `space-lg` (`24px`) on desktop and `space-md` (`16px`) on mobile.
- **Micro Rhythms:** Icon-to-text gaps within chips, status dots, and list indicators use strictly `space-xs` (4px) or `space-sm` (8px).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses frosted glassmorphism, surface translucency, and crimson edge diffusion instead of standard drop shadows.

### Surface Tiers
- **Base Canvas (Level 0):** Deepest background (`#070A10`). Plain, non-elevated ground plane.
- **Glass Cards (Level 1):** Translucent backdrop-filter cards (`background: rgba(13, 19, 34, 0.75)`, `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`), bordered by a hairline stroke (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`).
- **Hover & Interactive Layer (Level 2):** Elevated interactive state with brightening (`background: rgba(19, 28, 49, 0.85)`), border shifting to laser crimson (`rgba(225, 29, 72, 0.45)`), and a localized soft glow (`box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -8px rgba(225, 29, 72, 0.2)`).
- **Floating Overlays & Sticky Navs (Level 3):** Floating admission trigger ribbons, WhatsApp action nodes, and navigation headers (`backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`, `background: rgba(7, 10, 16, 0.88)` with `border-bottom: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`).

### Atmospheric Lighting
- Atmospheric radial gradient blooms in ultra-low opacity red (`radial-gradient(circle at top right, rgba(225, 29, 72, 0.12), transparent 50%)`) sit in the background behind primary CTAs and hero statistics.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a balanced, refined industrial curvature (`roundedness: 2`).

### Geometry Guidelines
- **Standard UI Containers (Cards, Modals, Sections):** Bound to `12px` to `16px` (`rounded-lg`), providing high-tech precision without severe harshness.
- **Interactive Controls (Inputs, Standard Action Buttons):** Engineered with `8px` (`0.5rem`) corner radiuses for clean ergonomics.
- **Pills & Metatags (Badges, Status Nodes, Live Pips):** Fully rounded pill geometry (`9999px`) to immediately signal high-priority status information, campus availability, and certification categories.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA ("Enroll Now", "Apply For Bootcamp"):** Solid `#E11D48` base, transitioning to a high-saturation crimson gradient (`linear-gradient(135deg, #E11D48 0%, #BE123C 100%)`). Text in pure white Plus Jakarta Sans SemiBold. Hover triggers an exterior glow (`0 0 24px rgba(225, 29, 72, 0.5)`) and slight upward translation (`-1px`).
- **Secondary / Ghost CTA ("Download Syllabus", "Campus Tours"):** Transparent background, `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`, text in `#F8FAFC`. On hover: `border-color: #E11D48`, `background: rgba(225, 29, 72, 0.08)`.
- **Icon Quick Action (WhatsApp / Admissions Hotline):** Circular or soft-square pill with high-contrast emerald or crimson status ring and glowing inner aura.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Indicators (`LIVE`, `CAMPUS OPEN`):** Pill-shaped with an active pulsing status light. Red tint (`background: rgba(225, 29, 72, 0.12)`, `border: 1px solid rgba(225, 29, 72, 0.3)`, text `#FF4D6D`).
- **Course Feature Tags ("AI Integrated", "NAVTTC Certified"):** Compact tags using `label-tag` typography in Geist, dark surface fill (`#111827`), subtle cool-slate border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`), and `#94A3B8` text.

### Cards (Course Cards, Campus Locations, Advisor Profiles)
- **Structure:** Frosted card (`#0D1322` at 75% opacity with 16px blur), hairline outline (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`), and internal padding of `24px`.
- **Course Card Layout:** Header houses certification chip & level badge; middle features strong H3 headline with course duration & lecture cadence; bottom pins the placement rate counter and immediate primary action.
- **Hover Micro-interaction:** Hairline border turns to crimson accent; subtle gradient illumination glides along the top border.

### Input Fields & Select Dropdowns
- **Input Ground:** `#0B0F19` with `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`. Height: `48px`. Corner radius: `8px`.
- **Text & Placeholder:** Input text is crisp `#F8FAFC`; placeholder text is muted `#64748B`.
- **Focus State:** Stroke brightens instantly to `#E11D48`, with an optical focus ring of `0 0 0 3px rgba(225, 29, 72, 0.25)`.
- **Dropdown List:** Dark floating surface with inset scrollbar, high-contrast hover selection in deep crimson wash (`rgba(225, 29, 72, 0.15)`).

### Metric Counters & Placement Highlights
- Numerical values render using `stat-counter` typography in pure white, accented by a crimson percentage or plus sign (`+`, `%`).
- Accompanied below by an uppercase two-line label in `Geist` (`#94A3B8`) providing verified trust proof ("18,000+ GRADUATES", "85% PLACEMENT RATE").

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Base box/circle rendered in dark slate (`#111827`) with `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`.
- Checked state fills with `#E11D48`, displaying a sharp white vector checkmark or concentric inner white ring with a subtle crimson outer shadow.
